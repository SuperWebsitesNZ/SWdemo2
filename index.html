Good questions

Straight answers.

What's the catch?

There isn't a monthly one. You pay for the build, and your site is hosted free, in an account in your name. Your domain renewal is the only ongoing cost, and it's paid to your registrar, not us.

What happens if you two disappear?

Your site keeps running. The website files, the hosting account and the domain are all yours, so any web developer can take over without asking us for anything.

Can I edit the site myself?

Not with a drag-and-drop editor. That's the trade-off for free, fast hosting. Most owners change things a few times a year, and at NZ$50 a change that still costs far less than a monthly platform.

Can I keep my current domain and email?

Yes. We check your domain and email setup first and make sure nothing breaks when the new site goes live.

What about bookings or an online shop?

We scope those separately and give you a fixed quote. Booking and shop tools often have their own monthly fees, and we tell you exactly what they are before you decide.

Preview notes

What changed in v2.

  1. Glossy logo on the site, flat logo for print.The header, footer and opening animation use the original glossy logo. The flat two-colour and one-colour versions below are for signage, shirts, invoices and anything tiny.The flat versions were rebuilt by eye and need a proper designer pass before print.
  2. One visual style.The layout uses a comic-panel look: thick plum outlines and hard offset shadows. No dots behind the heroes. The minimal studio-template look is gone.
  3. Type leans forward.Headlines are Barlow Condensed ExtraBold Italic, so they match the logo's slant and the heroes' motion.
  4. New comparison.Agency care plan vs. us, instead of Wix at NZ$30/month. The five-year gap is NZ$4,740 vs NZ$1,000 instead of NZ$1,800 vs NZ$1,500.NZ$79/month is an example from the middle of the NZ$40 to NZ$100 range NZ agencies charge for care plans.
  5. Hosting explained.New "Your keys" section and FAQ say where the site lives and what happens if you disappear.Assumes free hosting in an account the client owns. Confirm with Patrick.
  6. "No subscription" said once.The ticker and most of the repeats are gone. The claim appears in the hero and the trap section, with one honest footnote.
  7. Two clear build prices.One-page site NZ$1,500 and five-page site NZ$2,500, both + GST, each with an inclusions list. Bigger jobs get a fixed quote.Inclusions are a draft. Agree them with Patrick.
  8. Real work replaces the concepts.fancast.co.nz and cameracademy.com, plus a slot for one of Patrick's sites.
  9. Contact details.Email, phone and location now have a place on the page.Marked TBC until you two decide.
  10. Readable fine print.Nothing on the page is smaller than 13px. Short intro paragraphs now sit under their headings, not floating off to the right.
  11. No fade-in from blank.Every section is visible on load. The cost chart shows its totals straight away.
  12. Heroes in a middle style.Between the original glossy muscle-heroes and the flat friendly ones: glossy comic shading, athletic but natural builds, no masks, gloves, boots and silver trim back. Plain backgrounds behind them, no dots, no caption banners. Faces stay friendly and readable.
  13. Simpler steps and keys.The four comic chapter images are gone; "How it works" is now a path: four shield badges joined by a yellow line that ends in a key, each step with a timing.Day 1 / Day 2 / Weeks 1-2 / Week 3 are placeholders. Agree real timings with Patrick. "Your keys" is cut to three one-line points, with Keystone from the hero lineup as its guardian.
  14. Yellow buttons and a chain-break opening.Every button is now yellow. On first visit, a chain across the screen strains and snaps and the two halves pull apart to reveal the page. It works like the CamerAcademy title sequence: about 2 seconds, Skip button, once per visit, and off for anyone with reduced motion turned on.
  15. Share previews.Added Open Graph tags so links show a title, description and image on Facebook and WhatsApp.
